⬆️ Promotion Rules

  • Students must complete the required academic activities.
  • Students must satisfy attendance requirements.
  • Students must appear for prescribed examinations.
  • Required credits should be completed.
  • Backlog courses must be cleared according to university rules.
  • Students must satisfy the requirements for progression to the next semester.

🔄 Revaluation & Supplementary

  • Students may apply for revaluation when permitted.
  • Applications must be submitted within the prescribed deadline.
  • Supplementary examinations may be conducted for eligible students.
  • Students should register for backlog examinations separately.
  • Examination fees may apply according to university rules.
  • Results will be updated after completion of evaluation.

👩‍🎓 Student Code of Conduct

Students are expected to maintain discipline and professional behaviour inside the university campus.

Students should respect faculty members, staff, fellow students and university property.

Students must not participate in activities that disturb the academic environment or violate university rules.

Proper academic behaviour is expected during classes, laboratories, examinations, seminars, workshops and university events.

⚖️ Academic Integrity & Plagiarism

Students must submit their own work for assignments, laboratory records, projects and examinations.

Copying another student's work, submitting copied content without acknowledgement, impersonation, cheating or other forms of academic malpractice may result in disciplinary action.

Sources used in academic assignments and projects should be properly acknowledged.

📋 Course Registration

  • Students should register for courses within the prescribed period.
  • Students should verify their registered courses.
  • Prerequisite requirements should be satisfied.
  • Elective courses are subject to availability.
  • Changes to registration should follow university procedures.
  • Students should maintain a record of registered courses.

📅 Leave & Absence

  • Students should obtain permission for planned leave.
  • Medical leave may require supporting documents.
  • Absence should not result in attendance shortage.
  • Leave applications should be submitted within the prescribed time.
  • Long absence may require approval from the appropriate authority.
  • Students are responsible for completing missed academic work.

📢 Important Academic Notice

Students are responsible for reading and following all academic regulations, examination rules, attendance requirements and university notifications applicable to their programme.

Regulations may differ between programmes and may be revised by the university from time to time.
