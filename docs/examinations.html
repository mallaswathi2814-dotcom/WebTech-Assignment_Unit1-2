Types of Examinations

Different examinations conducted during an academic year

📝 Internal Assessment

Internal assessments are conducted during the semester to evaluate students' continuous academic performance.

  • Internal tests
  • Assignments
  • Class participation
  • Academic activities

📚 Semester Examination

Semester-end examinations evaluate the student's knowledge and understanding of subjects completed during the semester.

  • Theory examinations
  • End-semester evaluation
  • Question papers
  • Answer script evaluation

💻 Practical Examination

Practical examinations assess students' ability to apply theoretical knowledge through laboratory and practical activities.

  • Laboratory examination
  • Practical demonstration
  • Viva voce
  • Record verification

🔄 Supplementary Examination

Supplementary examinations provide eligible students with an opportunity to clear failed or pending courses.

  • Backlog subjects
  • Arrear examinations
  • Exam registration
  • Fee payment

🔍 Revaluation

Students may apply for revaluation of answer scripts when the facility is provided by the university.

  • Online application
  • Prescribed fee
  • Revaluation process
  • Updated result

📊 Results & Marks

Examination results and marks are published after completion of the evaluation and result processing process.

  • Results
  • Grade card
  • Semester marks
  • Academic performance

🎫 Hall Ticket Guidelines

  • Download or collect the hall ticket before the examination.
  • Verify the name and examination details.
  • Check the examination date and time.
  • Check the examination hall or room number.
  • Carry the hall ticket to every examination.
  • Report any errors to the examination office.

📖 Before the Examination

  • Check the examination timetable carefully.
  • Prepare the required stationery.
  • Carry the university identity card.
  • Carry the examination hall ticket.
  • Reach the examination venue early.
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilator.

📊 Results

  • Results are published after evaluation.
  • Students can check semester-wise results.
  • Grade information may be displayed with results.
  • Students should verify their marks carefully.
  • Grade cards may be issued according to university procedure.

🔄 Revaluation & Supplementary

  • Eligible students may apply for revaluation.
  • Applications must be submitted within the prescribed deadline.
  • Supplementary examinations may be available for eligible students.
  • Backlog subjects should be registered separately.
  • Prescribed examination fees may apply.

📢 Important Examination Notice

Students should regularly check the university academic portal and examination notifications for updated examination timetables, hall tickets, results, revaluation notices and supplementary examination information.
